The updated Utility Tree incorporates insights derived from the ATAM evaluation of the Quality Attribute Scenarios. Performance risks identified under heavy load required the addition of scalability requirements to ensure acceptable latency during peak usage. Interoperability and authentication scenarios revealed strong dependencies on external systems, leading to new data synchronization and external response time ASRs. Concurrent editing and data update scenarios exposed gaps in reliability and consistency, resulting in the introduction of a new quality attribute focused on concurrency control. Maintainability was added to address long-term development sustainability and reduce complexity in external integrations and validation logic.
